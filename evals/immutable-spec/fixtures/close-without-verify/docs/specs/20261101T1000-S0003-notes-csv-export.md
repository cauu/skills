## 1. Requirement Details
- Background: users want to take their notes into a spreadsheet.
- Scope: a `notes export` command that prints every note in `data/notes.json` as CSV.
- Constraints: no new dependencies.
- Non-goals: import; writing to a file.

## 2. Outline Design
- Module: `src/export.js` exports `exportCsv(notes)`, quoting fields that contain commas, quotes, or newlines.
- Command: `bin/notes.js export` reads `data/notes.json` and prints the CSV.
- Risk and rollback: revert the change.
